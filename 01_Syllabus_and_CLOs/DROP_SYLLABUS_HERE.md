# Drop the MEAI 502 — Applied Machine Learning syllabus in this folder

Place the official syllabus file here (PDF, DOCX, or MD), then tell me it is ready.

## What happens next

1. I read the syllabus in full.
2. I extract: description, credit hours, CLOs, topics, weekly schedule, textbooks,
   references, assessment structure, grade distribution, assignments, projects,
   quizzes, midterms, final exam, lab requirements, attendance, policies,
   CLO–PLO mapping, and accreditation requirements.
3. I fill in `../00_Course_Admin/COURSE_PROFILE.md`, replacing every
   `TBD — pending syllabus` field.
4. I propose any changes to this course's folder structure based on what the
   syllabus actually requires.
5. I build a preliminary semester organization in `../02_Semester_Plan/`.
6. I stop and wait for your approval before developing any teaching content.

## Ground rule

The syllabus is the primary source of truth for this course. I will not invent
assessment percentages, CLOs, policies, topics, or deadlines. Where the syllabus is
silent, I will flag the gap rather than fill it. Where you later override the
syllabus deliberately, I will follow your instruction and record the change in the
profile's change log.

## Also welcome in this folder

- Previous-semester syllabus (useful for comparison)
- Program specification / PLO list
- Accreditation or course-file templates
