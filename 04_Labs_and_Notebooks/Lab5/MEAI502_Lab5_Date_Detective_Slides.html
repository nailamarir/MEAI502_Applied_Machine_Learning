Concept · Why scaling?

Clues measured in very different units

Gradient descent takes one learning rate for all weights. With AREA in the hundreds of thousands and ECCENTRICITY below 1, the cost surface becomes a long, stretched valley.

Raw scales: zig-zag, slow
Standardised: straight to the goal
Press ▶ and count the steps.
Concept · StandardScaler

Put every clue on the same scale

z = ( x − μ ) / σ

μ = mean, σ = standard deviation, both computed on the training set only

After scaling, every clue has mean 0 and standard deviation 1. A "big" date and a "very round" date now speak the same language.

Inside a Pipeline, the scaler learns μ and σ from the train set automatically ✔

Step 7 · The full detective

The whole detective in 3 lines

# 1. scale every clue   2. one logistic regression per variety
detective = make_pipeline(
    StandardScaler(),
    OneVsRestClassifier(LogisticRegression(max_iter=1000))
)
detective.fit(X_train, y_train)              # learns μ, σ and 7 sets of θ
detective.predict(X_test)                    # the variety
detective.predict_proba(X_test)              # 7 probabilities per date

Why a Pipeline?

The scaler is fitted on the training set only, then the same μ and σ are applied to the test set. No information leaks from the exam.

One object

Fit, predict and save the whole detective at once, exactly what a factory needs.

Step 9 · A smarter detective

Which family of clues matters most?

🎨 Colour wins

Varieties differ strongly in colour (SAFAVI is by far the darkest). Sizes overlap a lot.

😮 Surprise

Colour only beats all 34 clues! More clues are not always better: noisy clues give the model more weights to fit with only 717 dates.

🌴 Case summary

What a detective (data scientist) does

  1. Translate the business request into an ML task.
  2. Check the data: clean ≠ correct.
  3. Always compare with a baseline.
  4. Scale your clues (inside a Pipeline).
  5. Multiclass with one-vs-all.
  6. Read the confusion matrix, not only accuracy.
  7. More clues ≠ better model.
  8. Use confidence to decide when a human should check.
